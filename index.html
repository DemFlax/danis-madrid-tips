OLD-SCHOOL MADRID

Madrid Super Classics

🐟

Fried cod & cañas

Casa Revuelta ↗

Order the battered cod with a cold caña. No ceremony, just proper Madrid.

🍷

Vermouth & callos

Bodegas Ricla ↗

Tiny, lively and old-school. Go for vermouth, callos and preserved seafood.

🥃

The legendary Yayo

Casa Camacho ↗

Order a Yayo: vermouth, gin and lemon soda. A true Malasaña institution.

🍸

Sherry time capsule

La Venencia ↗

Step back in time for sherry, olives, cheese and mojama.
